What it does

One app for cards, spending, budgets and bills. Read it like a statement: every line below is something CardSense does for you.

Statement of featuresCardSense 3.0
Home
Total outstanding, available credit, utilisation, this month’s spend, budget left and your next payment, on one screen, with a greeting by name.
Cards
Every card with its bank, credit limit, billing day and due day. Only the last 4 digits are stored.
Transactions
Log spends, refunds and payments. Search and filter by card, category, date or amount. Import a CSV from your bank statement instead of typing each line.
Budgets
A monthly limit for each category. You see how much a new spend will use before you save it, and get a warning at 80% and another at 100%.
Insights
Your biggest spending category, what changed since last month, cards running above 70% of their limit, and transactions that look unusually large.
Reports
Charts by category and month. Export to CSV, Excel or a printable report.
Notifications
An in-app inbox plus phone alerts on four channels you can switch on or off: payments, budgets and utilisation, activity, security.

A 4-digit PIN, fingerprint or face unlock, and a blank preview in the recent-apps screen.

Why

Credit cards are easy to use and easy to lose track of. Each one has its own limit, billing day and due date, and forgetting any of them costs money. These are the problems CardSense was built for.

A due date slips past.

Late fees and interest follow. CardSense checks every card each morning and reminds you 5, 3 and 1 day before the due date, and again on the day.

Your balance creeps toward the limit.

High utilisation can hurt your credit score. CardSense shows utilisation for each card, flags it above 70% and sends a notification at 90%.

The budget you set gets forgotten.

Every category gets a monthly limit. When you add a spend, CardSense shows what it does to that budget, and tells you at 80% and at 100%.

Two cards, two cycles, one headache.

Billing and due days are tracked per card and understand short months, so a due day of 31 lands on the 30th or 28th when it has to. You also get a note on statement day.

Money apps want your data.

CardSense does not. There is no sign-up, no server and no tracking. Android’s automatic cloud backup is switched off for this app.

How

Six steps from install to a month you understand. Setting up takes a minute; after that, CardSense does the remembering.

  1. 1

    Open it and say hello

    Enter your name, and a nickname or email if you like. No account, no password. A fresh install starts empty.

  2. 2

    Add your cards

    Card name, bank, last 4 digits, credit limit, billing day, due day and any amount already owed.

  3. 3

    Record what you spend

    Add transactions as they happen, or import your statement as a CSV. Indian DD/MM/YYYY dates are understood.

  4. 4

    Set budgets

    Pick a monthly limit for food, shopping, bills, rent or any category you create.

  5. 5

    Let it watch

    Payment reminders, budget warnings, high-utilisation alerts and unusual-spend notes arrive on their own.

  6. 6

    Review and back up

    Check insights at month end, export a report, and save a backup file somewhere safe.

When

CardSense earns its place on your phone at these moments.

You carry two or more cards

Different banks, different billing days, different due dates. One home screen with all of them.

The week a bill is due

Reminders start five days out and stop once the cycle is paid.

Before a big purchase

Check the available credit on each card and how close it is to its limit before you pay.

When the statement arrives

Import the CSV and check it against what you logged. No retyping.

At month end

See where the money went by category and what changed since last month.

Living on a student budget

Hostel, mess, rent, Uber-Rapido, cold drinks and gaming are ready-made categories.

Switching phones

Make a backup file on the old phone and restore it on the new one.

No signal at all

Everything works offline, including reminders and reports.
